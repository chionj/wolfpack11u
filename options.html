Wolfpack Chart Options

Real numbers from all 11 games, built from the GameChanger play-by-play. Each section shows a few ways to draw it. Tell me which option you want for each (for example "Hitting B, Situational A and B") and I will put it in the report. The game buttons below filter every chart.

Games
Every game has play-by-play data, so all four tabs use whichever games you leave on.

Where the kids play

Each position shows who has started there across the selected games. Click a position for the full list. Pick a kid to see only where he has played.
Position is the spot each kid started the game. Pitching is the exception: a kid who pitched is counted at P even if he started somewhere else. Mid-game moves in the field are not tracked, and a kid who started on the bench appears under "Started on the bench".

Position grid

The same counts as a table, one row per kid. Darker means more games at that spot.

Sort and filter

These controls drive all three options below.

APaired bars

Two bars per kid. Pick any stat for the left (blue) and right (green) bars to compare them side by side. Click a column heading to sort by it, and click again to flip the order. Without a click, rows follow the sort above.

BScatter plot

Every kid is a dot. Pick what goes on each axis. Dashed lines are the average of the kids shown.

CSlash-line strips

Each row shows AVG, OBP and SLG on one scale. A long gap from AVG to OBP means he gets on by walking; a long gap to SLG means extra-base power.
Pitching by lineup slot

AOutcome mix by part of the order

How our pitchers did against the top, middle and bottom of the other team's lineup. Each bar is 100% of that group's plate appearances.

BHeat grid by game

How often the other team got on base (hit, walk, hit batter or error), by game and part of the order. Redder means more. The fraction is on-base over plate appearances.
Baserunning and hits with runners on

ASteals by game

Stolen bases for us and for them, plus our runners caught or picked off.

BHits with runners on vs bases empty

Each bar splits a team's hits in a game by whether anyone was on base. Totals first, then game by game.

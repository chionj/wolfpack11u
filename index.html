How to read this. P/PA counts every pitch in finished plate appearances. FPS% is how often the first pitch was a strike (swing, take, foul or in play all count the same). 2K is plate appearances that reached a two-strike count and Surv% is how many of those did not end in a strikeout. Fl2 is fouls with two strikes (a hitter fighting a pitch off). K-S and K-L are swinging and looking strikeouts. Looking here means a called third strike only: the scorebook marks every strike the same way (called, swinging or foul), so strikes one and two cannot be split.
